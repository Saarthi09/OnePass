How it works

One identity. Every door. Three steps, and you decide at each one.

01

Connect once

Link the institutions that already hold your records. OnePass keeps permission to ask them, never a copy of your data.

02

Approve each request

A company lists what it needs. You see exactly what is asked, choose where each item comes from, and say yes or no.

03

Delivered, verified

OnePass fetches the facts, checks them, and hands them over stamped "Verified by OnePass". The company never learns which institutions you use.

Trust you can check

Companies rely on OnePass's stamp. The Stellar blockchain is what makes that trust checkable instead of blind. No personal information is ever written to it.

A public rulebook

The list of institutions OnePass may rely on lives in a smart contract. Every change is a permanent public transaction, so a fake university cannot be slipped in quietly.

A key nobody can forge

OnePass's signing key is published on Stellar. A company checks every stamp against it, without having to ask OnePass.

A receipt for every verification

Each approval leaves a timestamped fingerprint on the blockchain. It reveals nothing about you, and it can never be edited or deleted.
